Who processes data for us

  • Cloudflare (website hosting, waitlist storage, email forwarding).
  • An EU hosting provider (the KnowGauge app, its database and file storage).
  • OpenAI, USA (writing and checking test questions). Passages from your documents are sent to OpenAI for this; OpenAI does not use API data to train its models. Transfers to the USA rely on the EU standard contractual clauses.
  • An email delivery provider (account and invitation emails).

Cookies

The website sets no cookies and uses no tracking. Your light/dark choice is kept in your browser only. The app uses cookies that are strictly needed for signing in.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, and you can withdraw consent at any time, by writing to support@knowgauge.com. You may also complain to the Serbian Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection (poverenik.rs) or to the data protection authority where you live.

KnowGauge is in beta. Please do not upload confidential documents during the beta.
